# ⚽ FootballArena

**A football project built by a football lover, for football lovers.**

## 📌 About the Project

FootballArena is a personal project I started because of my love for football and my interest in building something around the sport.

As a football fan, I enjoy following players from different countries, comparing their performances, exploring clubs and leagues, and discussing who deserves recognition for their performances.

I wanted to create a platform where I could bring all these things together.

FootballArena allows me to add and manage football players in my own database, organize them by country, club, and league, explore player information, and give users a chance to vote for their favourite players.

My goal is to gradually turn it into a small football community platform.

## 🚀 What Can You Do With FootballArena?

- **Player Management:** Add, view, and manage football players in the database.
- **Clubs & Leagues:** Explore football clubs and different leagues.
- **Player Watchlist:** Keep track of your favourite players.
- **Voting System:** Vote for players you think deserve recognition, such as a Ballon d'Or-style Player of the Season award.
- **Community Polls:** A foundation for future fan polls and football discussions.

## ⚙️ How Does It Work?

FootballArena is connected to a database where player information is stored.

1. I add football players and their information to the database.
2. Players can be explored based on their country, club, and league.
3. Users can browse player profiles and keep their favourite players in a watchlist.
4. Through the voting system, users can vote for players they believe deserve recognition.
5. The platform brings player information and fan participation together in one place.

## 🛠️ Technology Used

- React.js – Frontend
- Tailwind CSS – Styling and responsive design
- FastAPI – Backend API
- Database – Stores player information and application data
- Axios – Communication between frontend and backend

## 🔮 Future Plans

FootballArena is still growing, and I have many ideas I would love to implement:

- **Real Match Fixtures:** Integrate real football schedules so users can see upcoming matches.
- **Match Results:** Display actual match results after games are completed.
- **Live Scores:** Show live scores and match updates.
- **Ballon d'Or-Style Voting:** Organize seasonal voting for the best football player.
- **Community Polls:** Let football fans vote on different topics, players, clubs, and football debates.
- **Player Statistics:** Add more detailed player performance statistics.
- **Football News:** Bring football news and updates into the platform.

## ❤️ Why I Built It

I built FootballArena out of my passion for football and my curiosity about web development.

It is more than just a CRUD project for managing players. I want to make it a place where football data meets fan opinions, where people can explore players, support their favourites, and participate in the football community.

**This is just the beginning of FootballArena!** ⚽
